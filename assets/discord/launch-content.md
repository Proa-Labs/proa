# Proa Discord launch content

## Welcome

Welcome to Proa — a place to build fast web applications in Rust.

Proa brings compiled components, streaming SSR, data loading, and fine-grained interactive islands into one framework. Whether you are trying your first component or shipping an application, you are welcome here.

- Get started: https://proa.so/docs
- Source and contributions: https://github.com/Proa-Labs/proa
- Explore apps: https://proa.so/ecosystem/apps
- Benchmarks: https://github.com/Proa-Labs/proa-benchmarks

Read #rules, say hello in #general, ask questions in #help, and share your work in #showcase. Use #feedback for ideas and #contributors for work on Proa itself.

## Rules

1. Be kind and constructive. No harassment, hate speech, threats, or discrimination. Critique ideas and code, not people.
2. Keep discussions relevant and use the appropriate channel. Beginner questions are welcome; give people time to answer.
3. No spam, scams, unsolicited promotion, mass mentions, or unsolicited support DMs. Share your Proa projects in #showcase.
4. Keep the server safe for work. Do not post explicit content, private information, credentials, or material you do not have permission to share.
5. Ask for help with context: what you tried, what you expected, your Proa/Rust versions, and a small reproduction. Redact secrets before sharing logs.
6. Report security vulnerabilities privately through the repository's Security tab: https://github.com/Proa-Labs/proa/security/advisories/new. Do not post exploit details in public channels.
7. Follow Discord's Community Guidelines and moderator instructions. Contact the server owner privately about harassment or moderation concerns.

## Announcements

Official Proa releases, noteworthy changes, and community news will appear here. Discuss updates in #general and share feedback in #feedback.

## Help

For the fastest useful answer, include:
- What you are building and what you expected to happen.
- Your Proa version, Rust version, and operating system.
- The smallest code example and exact error that reproduce the problem.
- What you have already tried.

Put code and logs in fenced code blocks, remove secrets, and keep follow-up discussion in a thread. Start with the docs: https://proa.so/docs.

## Showcase

Share what you are building with Proa: a screenshot or demo, a short explanation, a source link if available, and what you learned. Early experiments and work in progress are welcome.

## Feedback

Share problems, ideas, and feature requests. Describe the use case, the current friction, and what a useful result would look like. For reproducible bugs, open an issue at https://github.com/Proa-Labs/proa/issues and link it here.

## Contributors

Discuss implementation, documentation, examples, and contributions to Proa. Start with https://github.com/Proa-Labs/proa/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md and link the issue or pull request you are working on so others can follow along.
